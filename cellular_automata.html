How It Works:

  • Energy Propagation: Cells don't just live or die - they have energy levels (0-255)
  • Temporal Echo: Each cell remembers its state from 2 generations ago, creating interference patterns
  • Adaptive Decay: Energy decays faster in crowded neighborhoods, slower in sparse ones
  • Quantum Boost: When exactly 3 neighbors are active AND their combined energy matches certain patterns, cells receive energy boosts
  • Wave Interference: Current energy + echo energy creates constructive/destructive interference
